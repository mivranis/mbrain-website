01 / THOUGHT

Thought

Bring me the request. Let’s find the actual need.

A request often arrives with a solution already attached. “We need a new website.” “We need a platform.” “We need to automate this.”

I take it apart first. What problem are we solving? Who does it matter to? What creates value? What is necessary? What depends on something else?

The result is a business need that can be understood, challenged and justified.

02 / SHAPE

Shape

Now we work out what that need should become.

This is where the pieces come back together. Flows. Features. Services. Content. Technology. Options. Constraints. Priorities.

We explore the possibilities, make decisions and turn the business need into a blueprint that people can actually work from.

Something clear enough to build, and considered enough to go further than the original request.

03 / BUILD

Build

Then we make it real.

The blueprint becomes the website, the platform, the integration, the automation or the delivered project.

Sometimes I build it. Sometimes your team does. Sometimes several teams are involved.

My job is to keep the reasoning intact all the way through delivery, so what gets built still solves the problem we started with.

Services

What can we build from here?

You need the digital presence to live up to the thing itself.

Some projects deserve more than a perfectly functional website. The brand, the place, the product or the idea needs an experience with its own character.

I design and build creative websites around what makes the subject worth paying attention to, with the technology staying close enough to the concept that the idea survives implementation.

Your tools work. The gaps between them don’t.

Information gets copied. Tasks get repeated. Someone becomes the human connection between systems that should already be connected.

I look at the workflow as a whole, work out what should happen automatically and connect the pieces through integrations, APIs, automation and AI where it genuinely adds value.
