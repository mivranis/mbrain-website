04 / Digital Project Leadership

Some digital projects need an owner more than they need another hire

The work crosses teams. Decisions depend on other decisions. Suppliers need direction. Somebody needs to keep the whole thing moving.

I can step into a digital project for the period it needs me, bring structure to it and stay responsible for moving business, technology, suppliers and decisions towards delivery.

The problem

The project exists but ownership is unclear

A temporary ownership gap can become a permanent delivery problem

This service makes sense when a project is important enough to need senior attention and specific enough that adding a permanent role is unnecessary.

I work across the business and technical sides of the project, create the operating structure, surface dependencies and decisions early, and give teams and suppliers a clear route towards delivery.

Thought · Shape · Build

Project leadership starts before the project plan

01Thought

Make sure the project is solving a defined business need, the important decisions are visible and the stakeholders understand what is actually being delivered.

02Shape

Turn the work into scope, responsibilities, milestones, dependencies, risks, governance and a delivery structure that people can operate inside.

03Build

Coordinate execution, remove blockers, manage suppliers and decisions, and keep the project moving until the required result is actually delivered.
